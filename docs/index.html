About the data: sources, method and caveats

Sources

  • Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare (MHLW), Vital Statistics of Japan, final data, deaths (人口動態調査 人口動態統計 確定数 死亡), editions 2009 to 2025, tables 5-12, 5-16, 5-30, 5-31, 5-33, 5-34 and 5-35, via e-Stat.
  • MHLW, Overview of accidental death statistics, FY2009 special report (平成21年度「不慮の事故死亡統計」の概況), statistical tables 2 to 6, for 1995–2008 and for the 1995, 2008 age-specific rates.

Method

  • 1995–2008 values are taken from the 2009 report. 2009–2025 values come from e-Stat, using the newest edition that prints each year. Place and home tables are published one year at a time, so each year comes from its own edition.
  • Types follow the 2009 report: traffic V01–V98, falls W00–W17, drowning W65–W74, choking W75–W84, smoke and fire X00–X09, poisoning X40–X49. "Other" is the total minus these.
  • Road-user groups in figure 4 regroup ICD-10 transport codes the same way as the 2009 report.
  • Shares, changes and the 1995 index are worked out on the page from the published counts.

Caveats

  • In March 2019 MHLW recounted 2004, 2006 and 2009–2017 after some prefectures under-reported. Values for 2012–2017 here are recounted; 2004 and 2006 (from the 2009 report) and 2009 and 2011 are as first published. Where both versions exist, totals match.
  • From 2017 Japan codes causes of death with ICD-10 (2013 version). Falls and the split between choking subcodes changed at that point.
  • MHLW has not yet published 2025 death rates, so rates stop at 2024.
  • The share of non-traffic accidental deaths with no place recorded rose from about 30% in 2008 to over 40% in 2025, so counts by place understate every specific place.
  • Figures cover Japanese nationals who died in Japan, as in the vital statistics.
